Award May 27, 2026

Event of the Month — May 2026

KLE Technological University Student Branch (STB62711) won the IEEE NKSS Event of the Month award for organizing the outstanding "Technivex — IoT & Analog Hackathon."

Overview

TECHNIVEX was a intensive 10-hour hardware hackathon organized by the KLE TECH IEEE Student Branch (STB62711), aimed at promoting innovation, hands-on learning, and industry-oriented problem-solving among students through real-time hardware development challenges. The event featured two major domains: IoT and Analog, where participants worked in teams to ideate, design, and develop functional hardware prototypes based on real-world problem statements.

The hackathon encouraged creativity, teamwork, technical excellence, and practical application of engineering concepts in a high-energy competitive environment. Participants received mentorship, technical guidance, and evaluation from experienced academicians and industry professionals throughout the event.

To enhance engagement and spontaneous thinking, mid-hackathon giveaway activities were conducted where participants answered on-the-spot technical questions and were rewarded. Additionally, during the hackathon, teams were given surprise implementation tasks and design modifications to incorporate into their projects, and successful completion earned them bonus evaluation points.

The event witnessed participation from 118 students representing 7 different colleges and featured a total prize pool of ₹34,000. Multiple recognitions were presented, including the Sustainable Tech Award, Social Impact Hardware Award, Innovation Excellence Award, and Technical Excellence Award, encouraging impactful and innovative engineering solutions.

Objectives

  • To improve on-spot problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
  • To develop time management and task completion skills under pressure.
  • To encourage teamwork and coordination in a competitive environment.
  • To provide hands-on exposure to real-time hardware development challenges.
  • To enhance innovation, creativity, and technical implementation skills.
  • To spread awareness about IEEE and promote KLETECH IEEE.

Event Highlights

  • Successfully conducted a 10-hour hardware hackathon focused on IoT and Analog Systems.
  • Witnessed participation from 118 students representing 7 different colleges.
  • Encouraged real-time prototype development and practical problem-solving under time constraints.
  • Conducted mid-hackathon giveaway activities with on-spot technical questions and rewards.
  • Introduced surprise implementation tasks during the hackathon to test adaptability and innovation.
  • Provided mentorship and evaluation from experienced academicians and industry professionals.
  • Featured a total prize pool of ₹34,000 along with multiple special recognition awards.
  • Promoted innovation, teamwork, technical excellence, and industry-oriented learning.
  • Spread awareness about IEEE and highlighted the activities of the KLE TECH IEEE Student Branch.

Impact and Outcomes

  • Enhanced students’ practical knowledge in IoT, Analog Systems, and hardware prototype development.
  • Improved participants’ problem-solving, teamwork, and time management skills through real-time challenges.
  • Encouraged innovation and creative thinking by solving industry-oriented problem statements.
  • Provided hands-on exposure to project implementation, testing, and technical presentation.
  • Strengthened interaction between students, faculty members, and industry professionals.
  • Increased awareness about IEEE and promoted active participation in KLE TECH IEEE Student Branch activities.
  • Motivated students towards research, innovation, and future hardware-based project development.

Dignitaries and Judging Panel

The event was graced by the presence of Dr. Suneeta Budihal (HoD of ECE), Dr. Sujata Kotabagi (HoD of VLSI), and KLE TECH IEEE STB Branch Counsellor Dr. Sujata N. Patil.

The expert judging panel included:

  • Er. Vaibhav Sugandhi — Founder & CEO of Earthkey Innovation Pvt. Ltd.
  • Sachin Dalabanjan — Semiksha Semiconductor.
  • Assistant Professor Satvik Mathad — EEE Department, KLE Tech.

They were joined by other faculties and student members who witnessed the intensive hackathon competition.

Your dedication to innovation, teamwork, and technical excellence has set a benchmark for student activities across the subsection."
